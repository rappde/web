Compression Unit

Browser tool, uses your camera on request

Real-time video signal processing. Source is the live camera or a CRT filter. Controls for compression level, resolution, RGB offset, static noise, contrast and saturation. Saves stills as PNG or records video.

Displacement Unit

Browser tool, uses your camera on request

Displacement mapping for images and video. Source is the live camera or an imported map. Controls for X strength, Y strength and threshold. Saves stills as PNG or records video.

Open in its own tab Source on GitHub

Paint Unit

Browser tool

Digital painting on an imported source image. Three modes: Smear, Slide, Stamp. Brush size slider, canvas reset. Saves stills as PNG or records video.
